## Long description
(~4 700 chars — under the 5 000-char Amazon limit)

The empire is spreading like a plague across the galaxy, planting
outposts on every habitable rock it can find. You are an elite
rebel soldier, dropped onto those outposts with one job: wipe them
out. Three clones. Three lives. Three chances to give space a hug.

Space Huggers is a fast, responsive 2D run-and-gun built on a custom
physics engine. Every level is procedurally generated — the
terrain, the bases, the enemy patrols, the destructible crates, the
lava rocks that set you on fire — all generated fresh every time
you drop in. The map is the battlefield: shoot a metal crate, it
takes a while to break. Shoot a plastic one, it catches fire, the
fire spreads, and the whole pile goes up in flames. Roll into a
grenade stack at the right moment and use the explosion to launch
yourself onto a roof you couldn't otherwise reach. Every level
invites improvisation.

Five enemy types keep things varied. Green recruits hesitate and
go down in one hit. Blue soldiers are the backbone. Red captains
climb walls and flank you. White specialists are ninjas — they
roll, they jump, they close distance fast. Purple demolitions
experts hurl grenades and shrug off fire. Each one has a 5 % chance
to spawn as a Heavy, double the health, full-auto fire. Mix them
together and a single base becomes a five-minute problem that
demands every grenade you have.

Designed from the ground up for the couch. The D-pad moves,
jumps, and climbs; OK fires a single bullet per press; the media-
remote's Rewind throws a grenade and FastForward rolls. A real
Xbox or PlayStation controller keeps the game's native layout.
Pause with one button. Respawn at captured checkpoints. Up to four
controllers drop in for instant local co-op.

Originally built for the JS13K Games Competition in 13 kilobytes of
JavaScript, Space Huggers is a love letter to the 16-bit arcade
era — Metroid-style exploration, Contra-grade shooting, Smash TV
co-op energy. Native 1280×720 rendering with WebGL acceleration
and a Canvas2D fallback for older WebViews. Procedural starfields,
multi-layer parallax skies, rain, snow, fire propagation, blood
splatter, and eleven procedurally-synthesized sound effects
(thanks to ZzFX). 17 sprite textures, 12-color palette, one
big-screen-optimized build.

Features:
• Procedurally-generated planets — no two runs the same
• Fully destructible terrain with persistent state
• 5 enemy types + heavy-weapons variant (6 total)
• 7 different crate / barrel / rock types, all interactive
• Roll, grenade, climb, wall-jump, grenade-jump
• Capturable checkpoints for safer respawns
• 4-player drop-in / drop-out local co-op
• Pause, restart, next-level on the Fire TV remote
• Procedural sound effects — no audio files in the APK
• Procedural starfields, parallax skies, weather
• Internal 1280×720, upscaled to 1080p on Fire TV

No tutorials. No menus to memorize. Pick up the remote and you're
already playing. The empire is expanding — give space a hug.
